Ten individual student field projects supervised since 2023, alongside Summer Internship and Community Learning and Reflections mentoring.

Selected projects
======
* **Stuti Singh** (2026). Perceptions and attitudes of local communities living around Kuno National Park towards cheetahs and other wildlife.
* **Vanshika Malani** (2026). Heat, community, and everyday life: How do residents of slums in Kolkata use social infrastructure to cope with heat, and what factors shape access to these spaces?
* **Shubham Petale** (2026). Understanding the impacts of ecotourism on the local economy around Tadoba Tiger Reserve.
* **Vaishnavi Paliya** (2025). River into sewage: Degradation of the Swarna Rekha river in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh.
* **Deya Laha** (2025). Effects of urbanization on a rural area on the periphery of Hyderabad city.
* **Rachel Elizabeth Thekkanath** (2024). The sea, shore and its people: A study on the influence of coastal erosion on fisherfolk in select coastal villages of Thiruvananthapuram.
