Designed and taught. Offered January 2024, July 2024, July 2025, July 2026.

A lab-based introduction to spatial analysis for development research, built around open Indian datasets (SHRUG, NFHS, UDISE+, Bhuvan, IMD, OpenStreetMap) and QGIS. The course works deliberately across scales — district, village and point level — and treats the modifiable areal unit problem as a subject of study rather than a footnote.

Twenty-three sessions combining reading, discussion and hands-on labs. Lab worksheets are completed in QGIS and submitted through the course platform.
