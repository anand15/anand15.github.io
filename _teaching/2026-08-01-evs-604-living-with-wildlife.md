Designed. Under review, Course Review Committee, 2026.

A three-unit elective moving from human-nature relations and the histories of wildlife management, through agrarian and landscape change, to the appraisal of coexistence interventions. The course treats conflict and coexistence as historically produced categories rather than neutral descriptions, works across scales, and is grounded in Indian material throughout.

Three credits, 45 contact hours, fifteen weeks. Assessment through in-class case analysis, group presentation, and a staged term paper with peer and instructor feedback.
