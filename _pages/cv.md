Technical and methodological skills
======
* **Field methods**: behavioural sampling of free-ranging primates (scan, focal, all-occurrence, group follows, habituation); household and farmer surveys; semi-structured and key informant interviews; plot-based vegetation assessment
* **Quantitative analysis**: linear, logistic and multilevel regression; analysis of large administrative and survey datasets (Mission Antyodaya, Census, NFHS, SHRUG)
* **Spatial and remote sensing**: proximity, accessibility and network analysis; land-use classification and modelling; landscape characterisation; fragmentation analysis
* **Computational text analysis**: named entity recognition and annotation of ecological literature; corpus construction and text classification
* **Qualitative and archival**: thematic analysis of interview material; policy, legislative and archival document analysis
* **Software**: R, Python, QGIS, Google Earth Engine, ODK, KoboCollect
* **Languages**: Bhojpuri (first language, field research); Hindi (fluent; academic and popular writing, field research); English (academic and popular writing)
